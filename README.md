# Kaizen

![Diya](assets/diya.gif)

Kaizen is not a habit tracker. It is a digital ashram — a sacred daily ritual space for self-transformation through observation, reflection, and presence. The name comes from the Japanese philosophy of continuous improvement: small steps, every day, compounding into profound change. The deeper inspiration is the introspective practices of those who conquered themselves by observing themselves honestly — Buddha, Marcus Aurelius, Miyamoto Musashi, Swami Vivekananda. This app is not about crushing goals. It is about becoming unshakable, through awareness, not force.

## Step-by-step: how to use Kaizen

### First time only

1. **Open the app**  
   Go to [https://hhrishikeshh.github.io/Kaizen/#/](https://hhrishikeshh.github.io/Kaizen/#/) in Chrome.

2. **Onboarding**  
   - Read the welcome screen → **continue**.  
   - **Choose a folder** where your data will live (journal, habits, reflections). Everything stays on your device; nothing is sent to a server.  
   - Confirm the “one night at a time” screen → **continue**.  
   You only do this once (unless you reconnect a different folder later in Settings).

3. **Light the diya**  
   A short diya (lamp) animation plays. When it ends, you enter the main app.

### Every night (the daily ritual)

4. **Open the app**  
   Same URL. If you already chose a folder, the diya may play briefly, then you land on **Home**.

5. **On Home — do your ritual in order (top to bottom):**  
   - **Date and quote** — Set the tone.  
   - **Habit cards** — Log the day honestly: **Nourishment** (eating), **Movement**, **Reading**, **Meditation**, **Journaling**. Tap each to mark how it went (or skip). No judgment; observation only.  
   - **Today’s intentions** — If you set intentions for today on the **Plan** page, they appear here. Check them off as you complete or acknowledge them.  
   - **Last week’s reflection** — If you did a Sunday session, a short reflection card may appear; read it as you like.  
   - **Nightly journal** — Write in your own words. A rotating prompt is shown. Aim for a few honest lines (the app encourages a minimum of ~20 words).  
   - **Closing** — Choose how you feel (e.g. at peace, grateful, heavy) or type your own. Enter **one word for today**. Then click **“The day is complete. Rest now. →”**.

6. **End of session**  
   The diya extinguishes and the screen locks until the next day. You’re done. Close the tab and rest.

### Other pages (sidebar)

- **Habits** — View the last 28 days of habit and journal presence. Log today’s habits on **Home**; this page is for looking back.  
- **Plan** — Set **weekly intentions** by day (Monday–Sunday). These show up on Home as “Today’s intentions.” Use previous/next week to plan or review.  
- **Sunday** — For your **weekly AI reflection**. On Sundays you can copy a “package” of your week’s data, paste it into an AI (e.g. Claude), have a conversation, then paste back the session notes and a short reflection. The app stores that and can show it on Home.  
- **Growth** — Presence over time, journal arc, closing-words river, and **monthly / quarterly / half-year / yearly** cycles. Generate a package for a period, reflect with AI, paste back notes to build a longitudinal view.  
- **Settings** — Download a **backup** of your data, set your **name** (so the AI can address you), or **reconnect** a different folder.

## Using Kaizen to its full potential

- **Do it once a day, ideally at night.** The design is for a single, focused session: light the diya, log the day, write a few lines, close. Consistency matters more than length.

- **Be honest, not performative.** The habit toggles and journal are for you. The value is in seeing yourself clearly, not in “perfect” streaks.

- **Use weekly intentions (Plan).** Set a few intentions per week. They appear on Home so you can tick them as you go. They work as a compass, not a to-do list.

- **Do the Sunday ritual.** Every Sunday, open **Sunday**, copy the week’s package, paste it into Claude (or another AI), have a reflection conversation, then paste back the session notes and a short user-facing reflection. That reflection can show on Home and in Growth.

- **Use Growth for longer cycles.** When you’re ready, use **Growth** to generate monthly, quarterly, half-year, or yearly packages. Reflect with AI, paste back the notes. Over time you get a clearer picture of patterns and growth.

- **Back up your folder.** In **Settings**, use “Download backup” regularly. Your data lives only in the folder you chose; a backup keeps it safe.

- **Name yourself in Settings.** Enter your name so that when you use the AI (e.g. Sunday or Growth), it can address you and feel more personal.

- **Keep the ritual small.** A few minutes of honest logging and a short journal entry are enough. The diya doesn’t fight the dark; it just burns. Let the practice compound over time.

**The Diya**

The central metaphor is a diya — a small clay oil lamp. It does not fight darkness. It simply burns, warm and steady, and in its presence darkness naturally recedes. The app works the same way. You do not fight your bad habits. You observe them in the light of awareness, and slowly they lose their power.

---

**The Ritual**

The app is meant to be used once a day — ideally at night, like a monk concluding his day in the dim light of a cave. You light the diya, log a few honest things about your day (eating, reading, meditation, journaling, movement), write in your journal with rotating reflective prompts, and close. The diya extinguishes. It will light again tomorrow. This nightly ritual — small, honest, consistent — is the practice. The data and AI insights are byproducts of showing up.

---

**What You Are Building**

Over time you build a longitudinal portrait of yourself. Weekly planning becomes intentional living, not productivity hustle. An AI companion meets you every Sunday — fully briefed on the week’s data and its own private notes from past sessions — to help you reflect, recognize patterns, and deepen self-awareness. Monthly, quarterly, half-yearly, and yearly reflection cycles let the view widen. Progress compounds: the system tracks not just what you did, but how your awareness itself is growing.

---

**Who This Is For**

Someone ready to look honestly at themselves. Not someone seeking hacks or shortcuts, but someone willing to sit with what is true.

---

**A Note on the AI**

Every Sunday, the AI companion (powered by Claude) meets you for a session. It receives the week’s data and a private set of notes it has kept from your past conversations — a schema handoff that lets it remember who you are and how you have been changing. In plain terms: it shows up knowing you. The aim is not to manage you but to help you see yourself more clearly.
